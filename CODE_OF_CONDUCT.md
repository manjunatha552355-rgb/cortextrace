# Code of conduct

This community follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part in this project without harassment, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Respect privacy. Never post other people's data, logs or telemetry without their consent. This matters especially in a project that handles agent activity.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to [Manjunatha@aiandmlconsultants.com](mailto:Manjunatha@aiandmlconsultants.com). Maintainers review every report promptly and confidentially. Depending on severity, they may give a warning, remove content, or ban someone temporarily or permanently. Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may face the same consequences.
